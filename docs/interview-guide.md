# Interview Guide — Blinkit Category Discovery

**Duration:** 35–45 minutes (12 core questions)  
**Companion:** [`research-plan.md`](./research-plan.md)

---

## Opening (2 min)

- Thank participant; explain purpose: improve how Blinkit helps people discover **new** product categories, not just reorder staples.
- Consent: notes/transcript stored for portfolio research; pseudonym used in repo; may skip any question.

---

## Warm-up — current behaviour

1. **Last three Blinkit orders** — Walk me through what you bought and why. What was planned vs impulse?
2. **Category breadth** — Besides groceries, what categories have you *considered* buying on Blinkit but didn't? Why not?
3. **Shopping mode** — When you open the app, do you search, browse, or both? What triggers each mode?

---

## Discovery barriers (core)

4. **Awareness** — How do you hear about new categories or products Blinkit carries? What's missing?
5. **Trust** — Tell me about a time you hesitated to buy something unfamiliar. What would have helped?
6. **Price & value** — When does price stop you from trying a new category on Blinkit vs elsewhere?
7. **Search & findability** — Describe a product you couldn't find quickly. What happened?
8. **Habit & time** — What keeps your basket repetitive? What would make exploration feel worth the time?

---

## Recommendations & AI

9. **Past recommendations** — Have Blinkit recommendations ever changed what you bought? One example either way.
10. **Ideal nudge** — If the app suggested *one* adjacent item per order, where should it appear (home, cart, checkout)? What would make you dismiss it?
11. **AI appetite** — How interested are you in an AI assistant that knows your habits? What would increase or destroy trust?

---

## Jobs-to-be-done & wrap-up

12. **Job story** — Complete: "When I ___, I want to ___, so I can ___" for your Blinkit shopping.
13. **Single improvement** — If Blinkit fixed one discovery-related thing in the next month, what should it be?
14. **Anything else** — Surprises, pet/baby/health categories, small-town availability, family account usage.

---

## Moderator notes

- Probe for **specific moments** (last cart, last failed search) not general opinions.
- Capture verbatim quotes with timestamps or paragraph markers for coding spans.
- Flag segments: parent, pet owner, daily vs weekly user, explorer vs non-explorer.
- Avoid leading with "AI" before the participant names friction on their own (mirror survey Wave 2 ordering fix).
